## Tamil Nadu Irrigated Agriculture Modernisation Project (TNIAMP)
Source: TNAGRISNET GO & Scheme list (https://www.tnagrisnet.tn.gov.in/people_app/GoScheme)
Department: Horticulture / தோட்டக்கலை துறை
- **Scheme Description:** TNIAMP is a multi-disciplinary project funded by World Bank and implemented by the Government of Tamil Nadu. The main objective of the programme is to accelerate crop diversification from crops requiring more water to high remunerative and less water requiring Horticultural crops, through promotion of hi-tech cultivation technologies.
Tamil Nadu irrigated Agriculture Modernization Project is being implemented from 2018-19 to 2024-25 in 66 sub basins covering 34 districts (except Kanyakumari, Tiruppur, Nilgiris and Chennai)
In crop Demonstration component the emphasis will be given to diversification of Horticulture crops like Vegetables, Fruits, Flowers, Spices and Plantation Crops. Also encouraging Pandal Cultivation, Mulching, Shade net erection, Integrated Pest Management and Micro irrigation technologies with 100% subsidy to small and marginal farmers and 75% to other farmers.
In the year 2022-23, the scheme is being implemented in 54 Sub-Basins.
Model village concept is being implemented in Phase I (Third year) 16 districts comprising 61 villages and Phase II (First year) 12 districts comprising 34 villages.
- **Eligibility:** All districts (except Kanyakumari, Tiruppur, Nilgiris and Chennai)
Preference will be given to small and marginal farmers, women farmers and SC / ST farmers.
- **Documents Required:** 1.Chitta
2.Adangal
3.Passport size photo - 2 Nos
4.Photocopy of Ration Card/ Smart Card
5.Photocopy of Aadhar Card
6.Bank account Details

## Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee yojana (PMKSY) - Micro Irrigation(MI)
Source: TNAGRISNET GO & Scheme list (https://www.tnagrisnet.tn.gov.in/people_app/GoScheme)
Department: Horticulture / தோட்டக்கலை துறை
- **Scheme Description:** "Micro-irrigation technology is an effective technology that increases the water use efficiency by 40 to 60 percent by using water resources judiciously. This technology also increases the fertilizer use efficiency, reduces the wastage of fertilizers, controls weed growth and reduces labor cost significantly. By adopting micro-irrigation technology, the cultivated area can be greatly increased.
In Tamil Nadu, the Micro Irrigation Scheme is implemented from 2007-08 onwards. Micro Irrigation is being implemented through an online portal உயடடநன “Micro Irrigation Management and Information System-MIMIS”.
100 percent subsidy is being provided for small/marginal farmers up to 2 hectares and 75 percent subsidy is being provided for other farmers up to 5 hectares.12% GST applicable on Micro Irrigation System is also absorbed by the Tamil Nadu Government for the benefit of the farmers. 65 firms have been registered through TANHODA for the implementation of this scheme.
During the year 2022-23, this scheme has been proposed to be implemented in an area of 1,15,000 Ha at an outlay of Rs.960 Crore covering Horticultural and Agricultural crops."
- **Eligibility:** "All districts except Chennai
Preference will be given to small and marginal farmers, women farmers and SC / ST farmers."
- **Documents Required:** "1.Chitta

## State Horticulture Development Scheme (SHDS)-2024-25
Source: TNAGRISNET GO & Scheme list (https://www.tnagrisnet.tn.gov.in/people_app/GoScheme)
Department: Horticulture / தோட்டக்கலை துறை
- **Scheme Description:** To increase the area and production of Horticultural crops and to enhance the farmers income, State Horticulture Development scheme is being implemented with 100% State Government contribution at an outlay of Rs. 35.85 crore during the year 2024-25.
This scheme contains components such as Area Expansion of Horticulture crops, Promotion of Hi-Tech Practices in cultivation of Horticulture crops through protected cultivation - Poly Green House, Shadenet, Hydroponics and Vertical garden to meet out the demand of vegetables in Urban areas within the limited available space,Promotion of Homestead Garden,Promotion of Intercropping through Intercropping of vegetables with cashew, intercropping of vegetables with perennial Horticulture crops, intercropping of Nutmeg with coconut plantation,intercropping of Banana with coconut plantation,Need based interventions in Horticultural crops, Establishing Cottage Mushroom units,Establishment of Garden in Educational Institutions and Orphanages and Moringa Export Zone
- **Eligibility:** Farmers and individuals of all districts
Preference will be given to small and marginal farmers, women farmers and SC / ST farmers.

## National Horticulture Mission (NHM)-2024-25
Source: TNAGRISNET GO & Scheme list (https://www.tnagrisnet.tn.gov.in/people_app/GoScheme)
Department: Horticulture / தோட்டக்கலை துறை
- **Scheme Description:** National Horticulture Mission (MIDH - NHM)
National Horticulture Mission is being implemented with an objective of bringing holistic development of Horticulture sector in the State.
Focused attention is given to increase the production and productivity of Horticultural crops by adopting strategies such as Promotion of hybrids, diversification of traditional cropping system into Hi-tech protected cultivation, High density planting, Rejuvenation of old senile and unproductive Plantation/Orchards, Supply of quality planting materials, Encouraging pollination through bee keeping, Development of infrastructure for Post-Harvest Management, Crop specific programmes, Intervention of suitable scientific technologies through extension functionaries etc.
